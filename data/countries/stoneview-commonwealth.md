## Overview

The Stoneview Commonwealth governs the eastern highland and mountain states. Its rugged terrain and mineral wealth have made it prosperous but difficult to govern. The Commonwealth is formally Union-aligned, a relationship it uses to maintain a buffer against Veltoran and Smogspire industrial expansion into its mineral-rich highlands.

## Military

Commonwealth forces specialize in mountain warfare — sure-footed mech frames designed for extreme terrain, with a focus on long-range fire support from elevated positions.

## Relations

- **Smogspire Union**: Rival claimants to highland mineral rights. Tensions are high.
- **Farreach League**: Cautious respect. Neighbors who prefer to leave each other alone.
- **Veltora Federation**: Economic tension. The Federation wants unrestricted access to Commonwealth mineral deposits.
