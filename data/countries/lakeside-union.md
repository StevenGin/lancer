## Overview

The Lakeside Union governs the territories surrounding the great inland lake and the eastern coast. A trading nation that leverages its position between the interior lake and the Eastern Sea, the Union is commercially oriented and politically pragmatic. It maintains Union alignment primarily for trade access rather than ideological commitment.

## Military

The Union maintains a modest defensive force centered on lake and coastal patrol vessels. Their mech contingent is small, intended for port and facility defense rather than offensive operations.

## Relations

- **Farreach League**: Friendly. Trade in lake fish for frontier goods.
- **Stoneview Commonwealth**: Good relations. The Commonwealth highlands provide the Union with highland resources.
- **Southpoint League**: Close maritime partnership. Regular trade convoys connect the two nations.
