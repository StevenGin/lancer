## Overview

The Northreach Confederacy is a federation of agricultural provinces occupying the planet's northern mid-latitudes. Governed by a senate of elected provincial representatives, the Confederacy is one of the more stable and democratic governments on Vandyse. Its vast grain production makes it economically vital and politically influential.

## Military

The Confederacy maintains a defensive militia rather than a standing army. Their mech contingent is small but well-maintained, supplied primarily by NHP-licensed frames from Union contractors.

## Relations

- **Veltora Federation**: Tense but stable. The Confederacy resents Veltoran industrial pollution drifting across its farmlands.
- **Duneward Coalition**: Contested border. The Confederacy administers Iceview which the Coalition considers within its historic sphere.
- **Union**: Officially aligned. The Confederacy relies on Union trade contracts and diplomatic protection.
