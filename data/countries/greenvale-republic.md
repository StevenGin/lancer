## Overview

The Greenvale Republic is the agricultural heartland of Vandyse, producing the majority of the planet's food supply. A democratic republic with strong civil institutions, Greenvale is one of the more peaceful and prosperous nations on the planet — a fact that makes it a constant target for economic pressure from more powerful neighbors.

## Military

Greenvale maintains a small but professional defensive force. Their priority is keeping the agricultural infrastructure intact, so they avoid conflict wherever possible and rely on Union diplomatic protection as a deterrent.

## Relations

- **Brightmarch Union**: Uneasy neighbors. The Union wants guaranteed food access; Greenvale wants guaranteed military protection.
- **Northreach Confederacy**: Friendly. Both are agricultural democracies with aligned interests.
- **Veltora Federation**: Cautious. Greenvale fears industrial encroachment on its farmland.
