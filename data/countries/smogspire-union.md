## Overview

The Smogspire Union is an industrial city-state alliance in the northeastern highlands. Heavily polluted but economically productive, the Union has leveraged its mineral wealth and manufacturing capacity into significant regional power. The ruling council is dominated by industrial guild representatives.

## Military

Smogspire fields heavily armed industrial mechs — originally designed for mining and construction, they've been weaponized into capable if ungainly combat platforms. Their frames are rugged and cheap to produce.

## Relations

- **Veltora Federation**: Deep economic partnership. Smogspire supplies ore; Veltora supplies finished goods and technology.
- **Farreach League**: Contested border regions. Resource extraction disputes are ongoing.
- **Stoneview Commonwealth**: Rival claimants to highland mineral rights.
