## Overview

The Southpoint League governs the temperate southern islands, the most climatically pleasant region of Vandyse. The League has leveraged its neutral geographic position — far from the major industrial conflicts — into a thriving trade and diplomatic hub. Oakhaven hosts more neutral negotiations and trade summits than any other city on the planet.

## Military

The League maintains a modest naval force and a small mech contingent. Their strength lies in diplomacy and trade leverage rather than military power — attacking the League risks losing access to the equatorial trade routes it controls.

## Relations

- **Rustcoast Confederacy**: Strong maritime partnership.
- **Lakeside Union**: Regular trade convoys. Friendly relationship.
- **Most factions**: The League maintains neutral relations with nearly everyone, which is both its strength and its burden.
