## Overview

A coastal city on the Eastern Sea under Lakeside Union jurisdiction. Corvida is primarily a fishing and aquaculture hub, its fleets venturing far into the Eastern Sea. The city is frequently battered by storms rolling in off the open ocean, and its extensive storm shelter network is a source of civic pride.
