## Overview

A coastal city built on and around a massive coral reef formation, Coralhold is a major port and salvage hub for the Rustcoast Confederacy's southern territory. Structures extend out over the water on reef platforms, and the salvage markets here deal in everything from decommissioned mech frames to deep-sea mining equipment.
