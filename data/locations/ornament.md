## Overview

**Ornament** is a discreet desert city and the hidden seat of the **DSU**. Behind unmarked façades and blank doors, the planet's most influential people meet where no one can watch. To outsiders it is a quiet trade town; to those who know, it is where Vandyse is actually governed.
