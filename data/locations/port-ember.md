## Overview

Port Ember is the largest coastal city of the Rustcoast Confederacy and the primary naval and trade port on the western seaboard. The city's shipyards produce both civilian freighters and military vessels, and its markets handle a substantial fraction of Vanities' interregional trade. The famous seawall holds back both ocean surges and the worst of the acid rain that drifts from the industrial north.
