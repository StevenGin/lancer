## Overview

Named for the vast industrial chimneys that define its skyline, Smogspire is the capital of the Smogspire Union and one of the most heavily industrialized cities on the planet. The population lives beneath a permanent chemical haze; air quality inside the city varies from merely poor to outright hazardous depending on wind direction.
