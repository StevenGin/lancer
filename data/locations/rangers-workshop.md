## Overview

A fortified outpost and supply depot operated by the Farreach League's Ranger Corps. Located on the contested border between Farreach League and Stoneview Commonwealth territory, the Workshop serves as both a resupply point for rangers patrolling the highland frontier and a forward observation post watching for incursions.
