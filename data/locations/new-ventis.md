## Overview

**New Ventis** is the home of the **VLC**: a city of two halves, one that does the polluting and one that is the polluted. Its refineries crack the super-fuel that powers the Combine's fleets, and rumour places the paracausal pilot **Nova** somewhere behind the Combine Spire's sealed levels.
