## Overview

Not a fixed settlement, but the marked territory of the Saitan Clan — a powerful nomadic confederation that roams the deep desert of the Duneward Coalition. The Saitan Clan occupies an uneasy position: nominally within the Coalition's borders, they answer to no government and have repelled every attempt at forced assimilation. Their sand crawlers — enormous mobile fortresses — are legendary.
