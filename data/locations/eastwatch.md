## Overview

The largest city of the Stoneview Commonwealth, Eastwatch sits on a dramatic coastal cliff overlooking the Eastern Sea. Its lighthouse array guides shipping through the treacherous coastal reefs, and the citadel above has never been taken by force. The city serves as the eastern gateway to the highland interior.
