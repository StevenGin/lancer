## Overview

The administrative capital of the Lakeside Union, built along the shores of the great inland lake that defines the region. Lakeside's economy centers on freshwater fishing, lake transport, and light manufacturing. The Union Assembly building, constructed partially over the water on enormous pylons, is the city's most distinctive landmark.
