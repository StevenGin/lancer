## Overview

The primary city of the Southpoint League and a major hub for equatorial trade. Oakhaven benefits from the temperate island climate and serves as a neutral trading port for factions that cannot safely deal in the more politically charged northern cities. The city's name comes from the ancient oak forests that once covered the surrounding islands — most have been cleared for development, though the Research Gardens maintain carefully tended specimens.
