## Overview

A desert outpost city at the western edge of the Duneward Coalition's territory, Whiteholm sits atop one of the largest underground aquifer systems on the continent. Its water processing facilities supply the surrounding desert settlements, making control of Whiteholm politically significant far beyond its modest size.
