## Overview

Dunegate sits at the only navigable mountain pass connecting the Duneward desert plains to the central agricultural regions. Whoever controls Dunegate controls the flow of goods between the western desert states and the interior. The city has been fortified and re-fortified over centuries of conflict and currently sits under Brightmarch Union administration — an arrangement disputed by the Duneward Coalition.
