## Overview

The Rustcoast Confederacy governs the western coastal cities, from Port Ember in the north to Coralhold in the south. A maritime nation built on trade, privateering, and coastal industry, the Confederacy is pragmatic and commercially minded above all else. Their coastline is their strength — whoever wants ocean access must deal with them.

## Military

The Confederacy's military strength is naval, with a powerful fleet of coastal patrol vessels and a small but capable mech contingent focused on port defense and amphibious operations.

## Relations

- **Duneward Coalition**: Trade partnership. The desert states need coastal access.
- **Southpoint League**: Friendly maritime relationship. Rustcoast ships regularly call at Oakhaven.
- **Brightmarch Union**: Commercial ties, though the Union's land-based power limits mutual leverage.
