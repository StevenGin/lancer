## Overview

The **DSU** is the smallest and most secretive corporation on Vandyse — and quietly the most influential. Part think-tank, part secret society, its membership reads like a planetary MENSA crossed with the Illuminati: the people who actually decide things, meeting where no one can see them.

It fronts no armies and flaunts no wealth, yet the **Duneward Coalition**, **Rustcoast Confederacy**, **Brightmarch Union** and **Farreach League** all answer, eventually, to a DSU recommendation.

## Home Base

**Ornament**, a discreet desert city, hides the DSU's seat of power behind blank façades and unmarked doors.
