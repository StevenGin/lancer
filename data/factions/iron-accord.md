## Overview

The **Iron Accord** is a hard-bitten pact of frontier and coastal states forged from necessity rather than ideology. It binds the **Duneward Coalition**, the **Rustcoast Confederacy** and the **Brightmarch Union** into a defensive and resource-sharing alliance.

The Accord prizes self-sufficiency and is wary of both the Concord's idealism and Caladrius's orbital reach.
