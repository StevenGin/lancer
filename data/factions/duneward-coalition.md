## Overview

The Duneward Coalition governs the vast desert plains of Vanities' western continent. A confederation of city-states and nomadic groups bound by common need rather than common culture, the Coalition is fractious internally but presents a unified front externally. The Union Station at DSU Hub gives the Coalition disproportionate political leverage.

## Military

Coalition military strength lies in mobility — fast scout mechs and desert-adapted support frames. The Saitan Clan maintains their own independent military force that the Coalition cannot truly command.

## Relations

- **Brightmarch Union**: Dispute over Dunegate is ongoing and occasionally violent.
- **Rustcoast Confederacy**: Trade partnership for coastal access in exchange for desert resource rights.
- **Union**: The Coalition uses Union Station access as leverage in all negotiations.
