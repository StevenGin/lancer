## Overview

The Farreach League is a loose confederation of frontier settlements in the eastern reaches of the continent. Geographically remote and resource-poor, the League has survived through fierce independence, an exceptional ranger corps, and careful diplomacy that keeps the larger powers from agreeing on how to carve it up. The League's rangers are considered among the finest light-mech pilots on Vanities.

## Military

The League's military doctrine relies on mobility, guerrilla tactics, and intimate knowledge of their home terrain. Their mech frames are secondhand or salvaged — maintained to exceptional standards through sheer necessity.

## Relations

- **Smogspire Union**: Ongoing resource conflicts on the northern border.
- **Stoneview Commonwealth**: Tense but stable. Both are highland powers with mutual interests in keeping the larger nations out.
- **Veltora Federation**: Wariness. The Federation has made several economic overtures that the League has declined.
