## Overview

The **Highland League** governs Vandyse's rugged eastern interior. A loose commonwealth of mountain and lake states, it unites the **Stoneview Commonwealth**, the **Farreach League** and the **Lakeside Union** around control of the planet's richest ore and hydro resources.
