## Overview

The **VLC** is the top dog: heavy industry, super-fuel refining, an in-house pilot program, and the planet's only sanctioned link to ISPN paracausal research. Its flagship asset is **Nova**, a super-soldier whose existence the Combine neither confirms nor denies.

It controls the industrial core — the **Veltora Federation**, **Smogspire Union** and **Stoneview Commonwealth** — and the foundries that arm half the planet.

## Home Base

**New Ventis** is a city of two halves: one side does the polluting, the other side is the polluted.
