## Overview

The **Concord of Free States** is a democratic bloc uniting Vandyse's great agricultural nations. It guarantees mutual defence and free grain trade between the **Northreach Confederacy**, the **Greenvale Republic** and the **Southpoint League**.

The Concord is the planet's breadbasket and its loudest voice for Union-aligned stability.
