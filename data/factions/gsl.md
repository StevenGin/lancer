## Overview

The **GSL** controls what people need and what people want. It owns the food supply and the medical supply, and it runs the gambling, the loans and the rackets beneath them. On Vandyse, **everyone owes the GSL a debt** — and the GSL never forgets one.

It holds the agricultural and coastal heartland: the **Greenvale Republic**, **Northreach Confederacy**, **Southpoint League** and **Lakeside Union**.

## Home Base

**Lyric** is a vast dystopian city of neon and rain. Deals are struck in the **Pearl Towers** above and finalised on the floor of the **Mystique Casino** below.
