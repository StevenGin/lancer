## Overview

The Brightmarch Union is a militaristic state occupying the strategic central corridor between the desert west and the agricultural heartland. Control of Dunegate Pass gives the Union enormous leverage over continental trade. The Union produces its own mech frames — heavy, well-armored designs suited to holding fortified positions.

## Military

The Union's military doctrine centers on positional warfare and fortification. Their heavy mech frames are among the toughest on the planet, and their fortification engineers are second to none.

## Relations

- **Duneward Coalition**: Active dispute over Dunegate. Skirmishes are common.
- **Greenvale Republic**: Tense alliance. The Union provides military protection; Greenvale provides food.
- **Rustcoast Confederacy**: Commercial relationship — Union goods move through Rustcoast ports.
